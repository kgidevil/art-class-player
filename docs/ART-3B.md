# ART 3B — instructor mode (v0.8.0)
- Player drawing toggle opens a compact pen/eraser/move toolbar. Seven colors, adjustable width and opacity, undo, undoable clear, visibility, optional pen-only input. Canvas uses pointer events and coalesced samples when available; pressure affects width for pen input. Mouse and touch supported. Pen-only excludes touch/mouse but is not hardware palm rejection. Physical Apple Pencil/iPad unverified.
- Transparent annotation canvas separate from source and analysis; normalized image coordinates, same flip/zoom transforms. Eraser uses destination-out and cannot erase original. Each photo retains its actions during a lesson including previous/next, but annotations are not saved to disk and reset on new/repeated/recovered lesson or reload. Per-photo cap 250 actions and 2000 points per stroke; clear remains undoable within cap (oldest action may be dropped at cap). No export of annotation images.
- Instructor mode optionally displays actual next step (photo or break), step-based progress percentage and scheduled remaining duration, excluding future extensions and paused wall time. Presentation hides instructor preview, closes toolbars and selects move mode; drawing stays visible, may reopen tools explicitly. Same-screen mirroring only, not independent teacher/projector displays.
- Tests include normalized positions, bounded history, progress, browser mouse drawing, eraser, undo, per-photo retention, visibility, pen-only and synthetic pen input, presentation preview hiding. Windows CI pending. Actual touch, Pencil pressure, 3-hour projection and Pages activation remain to verify.
